Schedule future events for the current session and manage existing ones.

- add: schedule `message` to fire into this session. Pass exactly one firing rule: `every` (interval like `30m`, `1h`, `1d`; converted to cron and stored in cron form), `cron` (standard five-field cron expression, UTC), or `at` (one-shot; must be in the future). For `at`, prefer a relative offset from now over computing a timestamp: `now+<positive integer><unit>` with unit `s`, `m`, `h`, or `d` (for example `now+5m`, `now+90s`, `now+2h`, `now+1d`), or the clock form `now+HH:MM:SS` of three two-digit groups, hours first (00-99, may exceed 24), minutes and seconds 00-59 (for example `now+01:30:25` is 1 hour 30 minutes 25 seconds from now). `at` also accepts an absolute ISO 8601 datetime (naive resolves in the local timezone, zoned resolves to its offset). A relative offset resolves to its absolute UTC instant at add time; only the absolute instant is stored and echoed.
- remove: delete a schedule of this session by `id`.
- list: show this session's schedules with id, firing rule, next due time (UTC), and message.

Fires are not tool calls: at each due time a fired message is delivered into the session and you should act on it. All schedules are bound to the current session and are removed with it. Recurring fires evaluate in UTC; a recurring schedule survives each fire until removed, a one-shot fires exactly once.
